About Our Project

Our Goal

Our primary goal is to automate the attendance process using facial recognition technology. We aim to provide a reliable, efficient, and user-friendly system that eliminates the need for manual attendance tracking, saving valuable time for both students and faculty.

Our Vision

We envision a future where educational institutions can leverage the power of artificial intelligence to streamline administrative tasks. Our vision is to create a comprehensive attendance management system that is not only accurate but also provides valuable insights into student attendance patterns.

Technology Used

  • Face Recognition Library
  • Eye Blink Detection for liveness Check
  • OpenCV
  • Dlib
  • Python & Flask
